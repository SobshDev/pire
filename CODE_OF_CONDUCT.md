# Code of Conduct

pire is a place to learn, and people learn best when they aren't afraid to ask a basic question or be
wrong in public. This code applies to everyone in the project's spaces: issues, pull requests,
discussions, and any chat or event run for pire.

## What we expect

- Be kind and patient, especially with beginners. Every reverse engineer once didn't know what RIP was.
- Critique the work, not the person. Explain why, and suggest what would be better.
- Assume good faith, and accept correction gracefully when you're the one who got it wrong.
- Respect people's time, privacy, and boundaries.

## What isn't acceptable

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks, in public or in private.
- Discriminatory language or behavior based on who someone is, including their age, body, disability,
  ethnicity, gender identity or expression, experience level, nationality, appearance, race,
  religion, or sexual identity and orientation.
- Sexual language or imagery, and unwelcome attention of any kind.
- Publishing someone's private information without their permission.
- Using the project to help attack systems or software you aren't authorized to test. pire teaches
  analysis on programs built for it; keep it that way in project spaces.
- Continuing any of the above after being asked to stop.

## Reporting

Email **gabriel@mentem.app** with what happened, where, and any links or screenshots. Reports are kept
confidential, and you'll get a reply within a week. If the report is about the maintainer, say so, and
it will be handled by someone else in the project wherever possible.

## Enforcement

Maintainers decide how to respond, in proportion to what happened: a private note, a public warning, a
temporary ban from project spaces, or a permanent ban. They may remove comments, commits, issues, and
other contributions that break this code.

This code draws on the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/).
